Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

ClipBot is a personal tool run by one person to post videos to their own YouTube and Instagram accounts. It has no other users.

What it accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner's channel and to read public view counts. ClipBot uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Instagram: permission to publish Reels to the owner's account and read their view counts.

What it stores

Login tokens and post statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to anyone else. ClipBot does not collect data about anyone other than its owner.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google account permissions or from Instagram's settings under Apps and websites.
